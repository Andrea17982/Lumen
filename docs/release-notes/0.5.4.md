# Lumen 0.5.4 — Orchestra keeps its plan and survives rate limits

> Historical release notes. For the current product, read [Features](../features.md), [Validation](../validation.md) and the [latest changelog](0.7.0.md).

Orchestra now runs subtasks on the providers its plan chose, and a rate limit during the final check no longer stops the run.

## Fixes

- **The plan's split is kept.** When Claude's quota had not been checked recently, Orchestra ran one subtask on it and moved the rest of Claude's work to ChatGPT. The planned account is now kept: its first subtask checks the quota, and the others wait for that check (about 20 seconds without a rate limit) instead of moving to another provider. Work still moves on a real rate limit, a quota over your threshold, or an account that is unavailable.
- **Moved subtasks show their model.** A subtask moved by the scheduler shows the model too, for example "ChatGPT · GPT-6.1-Sol", not just "ChatGPT".
- **Final check continues after a rate limit.** If the orchestrator runs out of quota during the final check or a revision, the other provider takes over and finishes the run. A note in the reply says so.
- **Audit skipped on a rate limit.** If the auditor runs out of quota, the audit is skipped ("Skipped: rate limit") and the checked answer is kept, instead of the run failing.
- **No endless spinner.** If no other provider is available, the final check is marked as failed instead of spinning forever.

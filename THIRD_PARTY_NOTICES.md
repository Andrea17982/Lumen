## Asset provenance

`public/lumen.svg` is original code-authored geometry generated by AI for this project. `src-tauri/icons/` contains raster/platform derivatives generated from that SVG by the Tauri CLI icon command. No generated photographic/illustrative raster assets or externally licensed fonts are shipped. Screenshots under `docs/images/` are captures of the implemented Lumen UI, not design mockups or evidence of live provider inference.
